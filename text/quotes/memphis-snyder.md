*“I love the trips; camping, backpacking, day hikes, field trips, and more. There is something special about the interactions and fellowship that springs from these escapades that is invaluable. Every trip I have been on has been a fond memory to look back on, from way back to my first backpacking trip to the most recent trip.* **As time has gone on, life has started to get in the way of these once frequent adventures, and with my time in high school coming to an end in less than a year, I want to be able to have one last big trip: go out with a bang, to put it simply.** *While my focus is not the college tours themselves, and rather the connection the trip itself brings, I also think the tours themselves will be more intriguing than I imagine currently.”*
